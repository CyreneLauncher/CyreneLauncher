## Confirmed crash and fix

At 10:33:38, Sodium Extra 0.9.4 fails during late config registration because
`SDL_GL_GetSwapInterval(IntBuffer)` is an unsupported facade stub. The preceding
`SDL_GL_GetCurrentContext()` now succeeds. Sodium Extra saves the interval, probes
`SDL_GL_SetSwapInterval(-1)`, and restores the saved interval.

The deployed Orbis EGL platform leaves `min = max = default = 1`; VideoOut VSync
and WSI producer throttling pin presentation to interval 1. The getter reports 1
for a current SDL context. The setter rejects negative adaptive intervals and
propagates the existing native PRX result. Nonnegative requests retain EGL's
clamping behavior. No JDK or native ABI change is required.
